# ChitraData • Staying Relevant in an AGI World (Without Cloud)

Focus on the handful of upgrades that keep our free, local-first experience worth the detour—even when AGI tools exist.

## 1. Storytelling Craft on Autopilot
- Curated chart + narrative templates (board update, growth dashboard, policy memo)
- Guided narration mode: headline prompt, supporting metric, call-to-action slots
- Insight callouts/annotations ready to drop on any chart or map

## 2. Aesthetic Excellence Out of the Box
- Premium palette library (WCAG-safe light/dark pairs, gradient sets, brand-ready themes)
- Layout presets for slides, social cards, and multi-chart dashboards
- Export overlays (presentation frames, watermarking toggle, social share frames)

## 3. Supercharged Offline Utility
- Pack/unpack projects as portable bundles (JSON + assets) to reopen later without cloud
- Optional desktop/PWA version so teams can run the studio entirely offline
- Bulk export queue for delivering multiple charts/maps in one go

## 4. Domain Packs That Encode Expertise
- Public dataset packs (India census, economic indicators, policy metrics) pre-formatted for storytelling
- Map overlays beyond India (states/regions we can legally ship) with best-practice palettes
- “Compare states/segments” wizards that surface useful defaults instead of raw numbers

## 5. Community & Transparency
- Open template marketplace: accept community PRs for new layouts, palettes, datasets
- Public roadmap + issue voting to keep power users invested
- Inline feedback hooks so users can request improvements without leaving the app

Deliver these pillars and ChitraData stays differentiated: craft, clarity, and credibility—without needing cloud infrastructure or paid plans.
